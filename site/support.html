Support

Help for Spotless on Mac and iPhone.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/fjenn/spotless/issues. A free GitHub account is enough. Please include:

  • what you did, and what happened instead;
  • Mac or iPhone, and the Spotless version (Settings > About);
  • a screenshot, if something looks wrong.

On the Mac, Settings > About can copy your system details for the report. Check them before you paste: they contain no account data, but they are yours to share.

Before opening a new issue, search the existing ones: someone may have reported it already.

Common fixes

  • A song will not play, or playback stops working. YouTube changes things from time to time. Make sure you have the latest version: the Mac app updates itself, and you can check under Settings > About. Try again a little later if it persists, and report it.
  • "Spotless can't be opened" on the Mac. Download the DMG from the official releases page only, and move the app to Applications before opening it.
  • No lyrics for a song. Not every song has lyrics in the public lyrics services. You can search for them manually from the lyrics view.
  • Your library after reinstalling. Your library lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. If you were signed in to YouTube, your YouTube library comes back when you sign in again.
