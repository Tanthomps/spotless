Privacy

Applies to Spotless for Mac and iPhone. Last updated November 2026.

In short: Spotless does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. The developer runs no server and receives nothing from the app. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your library: playlists, folders, liked songs, followed artists, shows and audiobooks.
  • Your listening history, listening stats and the recommendations made from them. They are computed on the device.
  • Downloads, settings, and the app's caches.

This data is never uploaded by Spotless. Deleting the app deletes it.

Services the app contacts

Spotless has no backend of its own. To play music and show information, it sends requests directly from your device to these third-party services. Each one receives only what the request needs, plus your IP address, as with any website you visit. Their own privacy policies apply.

  • YouTube / Google: search terms, song and video identifiers, to find and stream music and videos.
  • Lyrics services (such as LRCLIB and other public lyrics providers): the title, artist, album and length of the song whose lyrics you open or download.
  • Metadata services (Deezer's public search, MusicBrainz): song titles and artist names, to mark explicit songs and to find genres.
  • Podcasts: Apple's public podcast directory (search terms, followed show titles, your country code), the feeds and audio files of shows you follow (served by their publishers, which may use their own measurement services), and SponsorBlock (a short hash prefix of the episode's video identifier, not the identifier itself).
  • Audiobooks: LibriVox and the Internet Archive (search terms and book identifiers).
  • Playlist import: only when you import a playlist link, the service that link points to.
  • Updates (Mac): GitHub, to check for and download a new version.

None of these requests contain an account, an advertising identifier or a device identifier created by Spotless.

Optional YouTube sign-in

You can sign in with your YouTube account to see your YouTube Music library in Spotless. You do not have to.

  • Sign-in happens on Google's own page inside the app. Spotless never sees your password.
  • The sign-in cookie is stored in your device's Keychain. In the rare case the Keychain refuses to store it, it is kept in the app's local database instead. The sign-in page's own cookies stay in the app's local web storage.
  • The cookie is sent only to YouTube and Google, never to any other service and never to the developer.
  • While you are signed in, songs you play are added to your YouTube watch history, as in YouTube's own apps. You can turn this off in Settings, or use a private session.
  • Signing out removes the stored cookie.

Diagnostics

Spotless keeps a log file on your device to help fix problems. It is never sent anywhere. If you report a bug, you can choose to copy diagnostic details and paste them into your report yourself.

Children

Spotless collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/fjenn/spotless/issues.
